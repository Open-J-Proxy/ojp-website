Frequently Asked Questions

What is the response time?

For community support via GitHub, response times vary based on community availability. Enterprise support customers receive guaranteed response times based on their SLA.

Do you offer training?

Yes! We offer training sessions for enterprise customers covering OJP architecture, deployment, configuration, and best practices.

Can you help with migration?

Absolutely. Our team can assist with planning and executing your migration to OJP, including architecture review and implementation support.

Do you provide custom development?

Yes, we offer custom development services for enterprise customers requiring specific features or integrations.

What about security issues?

For security vulnerabilities, please email security@openjproxy.org. We take security seriously and respond to reports promptly.

How can I contribute?

We welcome contributions! Check out our GitHub repository for contribution guidelines, or reach out to discuss how you can help.
